# Consent form for face captures

Project: face liveness and presentation attack detection for the MOSIP Registration Client, MOSIP Decode 2026, problem statement 04.
Team: Best_Team, Cotonou, Benin.

Print this form, fill it in and sign it before the first capture. The signed form is kept by the team coordinator, on paper or as a scan outside the Git repository. It is never committed.

## 1. Purpose

The team builds a check that tells a live face from a photo, a screen, a video or a mask. To set its thresholds and to measure its error rates, the team needs short recordings of real faces and of attacks made with photos of the same faces.

The recordings are used only to calibrate and test the face liveness check of this project, for MOSIP Decode 2026. They are not used for any other purpose, such as identification, face recognition outside the tests of the project, or training a model for a third party.

## 2. What is recorded

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Video sequences of your face | Sequences of about 10 seconds from a webcam, stored as JPEG frames. You look at the camera, and in some sequences you blink, smile or turn your head on request |
| Attack artefacts made from your photo | A photo of your face is printed on paper, shown on a phone or laptop screen, replayed as a video, or cut out as a paper mask, and then presented to the camera |
| Technical data | Capture type, lighting condition, camera, resolution, date and time of the sequence |
| Skin tone category, self-reported | Optional. Used only to check that the error rates do not differ between groups of people. You choose the category yourself, or leave it blank |

Your name is not stored with the recordings. Each participant receives a pseudonym, for example `S01`. The link between your name and your pseudonym appears only on this form.

## 3. Storage and access

- Recordings are stored only on the computers of the four team members, in a folder outside the Git repository (`captures/`, ignored by Git).
- They are never committed to the repository, never uploaded to a public service, and never shown in the project video, the pitch, the documentation or the submission.
- Only aggregated figures are published: error rates, counts of sequences, processing times. No published figure allows a person to be recognised.
- Artefacts made from your photo (prints, masks) are kept by the team member who made them and destroyed at the same time as the recordings.

## 4. Retention and deletion

- Recordings, artefacts and the photos used to make them are kept until the end of the evaluation of MOSIP Decode 2026, and at the latest until **31 December 2026**. They are then deleted from every computer and the printed artefacts are destroyed.
- You can withdraw your consent and ask for deletion at any time, without giving a reason. Ask the team coordinator. Your recordings and artefacts are deleted from every computer within 7 days, and you receive a confirmation.
- Results already computed from your recordings are aggregated and cannot be traced back to you. They are recomputed without your recordings when the next results are produced.
- Withdrawal has no consequence for you.

## 5. Participation

Participation is voluntary. You can stop a session at any time. You can refuse any capture type, for example the paper mask, and still take part in the others.
